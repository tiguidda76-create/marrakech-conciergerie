---
title: Hospitality Dashboard Design
description: UI/UX patterns for property management dashboards
---

## KPI Cards Layout
- 4 cards top row on desktop, 2x2 grid on mobile
- Metrics: Occupancy %, Monthly Revenue, Avg Rating, Properties Count
- Sparkline mini-chart in each card showing 30-day trend

## Calendar View
- Month view default, week view toggle
- Color coding: booked (red), available (green), cleaning (yellow), blocked (gray)
- Click date → quick-add reservation modal

## Property List
- Card layout with: photo, name, location, status badge, occupancy bar
- Filters: quartier, type, status, revenue range
- Sort: revenue desc, occupancy asc, name asc

## Financial Charts
- Revenue: bar chart by month (MAD)
- Occupancy: line chart vs last year
- Source pie chart: Airbnb, Booking.com, Direct, Other
