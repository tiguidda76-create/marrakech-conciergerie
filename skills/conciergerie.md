## Check-in / Check-out Procedures
1. Verify guest ID and reservation confirmation
2. Collect tourist tax (11 MAD/night/person in Marrakech)
3. Provide WiFi credentials, house manual, emergency contacts
4. Explain AC/heating, pool rules, garbage schedule
5. Check-out: inspect damages, collect keys, review on tablet

## Cleaning Standards
- Change all linens (beds, towels, kitchen)
- Restock: toilet paper, soap, coffee, water bottles
- Check appliances: AC, fridge, WiFi router
- Photos before/after for damage claims
- Turnaround time: 3 hours minimum

## Pricing Strategy by Season
- High (Oct-Apr): +40% base rate
- Medium (May-Jun, Sep): base rate
- Low (Jul-Aug, Ramadan): -20% base rate
- Events (NYE, Marrakech Film Festival): +100%

## Legal Requirements
- Register with Délégation Régionale du Tourisme
- Collect and remit tourist tax monthly
- Insurance: responsabilité civile professionnelle
